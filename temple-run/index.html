HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

Desktop / Laptop

  • Lane Left: ← or A
  • Lane Right: → or D
  • Jump: ↑, W, or Space
  • Slide: ↓ or S
  • Pause: P or Esc

Mobile / Tablet

  • Swipe Left / Right: Switch Lanes
  • Swipe Up: Jump Over Obstacles
  • Swipe Down: Slide Under Obstacles
  • On-Screen Touch Buttons: Ergonomic thumb D-pad & action triggers

⚠️ Temple Hazards & Survival

🪵
Low Spiked Barrier:

Press Jump (Swipe Up) to leap over.

🏛️
Overhead Stone Lintel:

Press Slide (Swipe Down) to duck underneath.

🗿
Ancient Stone Totem:

Change lanes to avoid fatal impact.

🕳️
Crumbling Road Pit:

Jump across or switch to a solid lane.

🪙
Gold Coins:

Collect to boost your score (+10 pts each)!
